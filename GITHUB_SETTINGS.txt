## Short Description (for GitHub repo header)
🐢🚀 Educational SpaceX launch tracker for PVPV/Rawlings Elementary School Sea Turtles. Real-time rocket launches with ocean-themed design. "Surfing to Success - From the Ocean to the Stars!"

## About Section (longer description)
An interactive SpaceX launch tracker designed for elementary school students at PVPV/Rawlings Elementary in Florida. Features real-time launch data, countdown timers, and educational content connecting sea turtle conservation with space exploration. Built with Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS. Perfect for STEM education and classroom use.
